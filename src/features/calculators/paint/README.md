# Paint calculator assumptions

- Wall area uses room perimeter × wall height × room count. Door and window quantities are project totals, and their opening areas are deducted once from the total wall area before coats and coverage are applied.
- Ceiling, painted doors, trim/baseboards, and primer are estimated independently so users can choose different products and coat counts.
- Default finish and primer coverage is 400 ft² per US gallon. This is an editable planning value, not a product guarantee.
- The surface-condition choice provides guidance only; it does not apply an undisclosed multiplier.
- Purchase recommendations compare common US retail sizes: 5 gallons, 1 gallon, and 1 quart. They minimize purchased volume first and container count second.
- Cost estimates include only the container prices entered by the user. Labor, tax, supplies, and local price adjustments are excluded.
